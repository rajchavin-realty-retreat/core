Co-Founder & COO

Strategic Planner Operations Lead

The Execution Architect

Mr. Santosh S. Shidling is the Co-Founder and Chief Operating Officer (COO) of Rajchavin, bringing over 16 years of experience in sales leadership, business development, and pan-India operations. Beginning his career in 2008 as a Service In-Charge, he leveraged his deep technical foundation in electronics to transition into sales in 2013, mastering the art of solution selling.

Since 2016, he has led nationwide business development initiatives, driving market entry and distributor networks across diverse sectors. At Rajchavin, he oversees execution strategy, optimizing processes to build scalable systems that ensure predictable revenue growth and long-term stability.
