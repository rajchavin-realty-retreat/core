Property Transaction Services

The Rajchavin
Real Estate System.

We facilitate the buying, selling, leasing, and renting of Residential, Plot, Land, Commercial, and Industrial properties with absolute transparency, zero fund custody, and strict governance.

End-to-End Governance

Rajchavin is never a transacting party. We are the facilitation engine that connects buyers, sellers, and tenants through a clean, auditable, and traceable process.

Buy

Discover verified residential, commercial, and industrial properties with compliance-first documentation.

Sell

List your property with us. We handle buyer coordination and ensure a smooth, secure registration flow.

Rent

Standardized residential rental agreements ensuring fairness and legal clarity for both owners and tenants.

Lease

Commercial and industrial leasing executed with rigorous corporate standards and one-time transparent fees.

Master Pricing Policy

The 0.99% Revolution.

We believe real estate services must be simple, fair, and accountable. That is why our charges are lower than traditional brokerage, completely transparent, and billed only on success.

Buy & Sell (Any Property)

0.99%

Of the total transaction value. If referred through agents, we charge 0.49% from each agent.

Commercial & Industrial Lease

0.99%

Of the total lease agreement value. This is a one-time charge at lease execution with no recurring commissions.

Residential Rental

1 Month

Charged to both Owner & Tenant. Payable only after a successful agreement and occupancy.

Standard Payment Structure

  • 50%
    At Sale Agreement / Token Stage

    Advance fees to initiate processing (Non-refundable).

  • 50%
