Execution with Governance

Design & Construction.

Rajchavin does not act as a direct contractor. Instead, we provide the ultimate governance layer—coordinating, managing, and governing your execution through verified partners to ensure safety and quality.

The Governance & Coordination Model

We eliminate the chaos of construction by acting as your dedicated project coordinator. As per our UGRF, we maintain strict oversight while liabilities remain with the specialized contractors.

Not A Contractor

Rajchavin does not construct directly. We protect your interests by governing execution through a network of verified architects and contractors.

Project Management

From the first blueprint to the final coat of paint, we manage the timelines, vendor coordination, and design approvals.

Liability Clarity

For complete transparency, contractor liability and construction defect responsibility strictly rest with the executing partners, not Rajchavin.

Our Facilitation Scope

From Blueprint to Reality.

Design & Planning

Coordination of 2D Layout Plans, compliance structuring, and highly detailed 3D Elevations.

Turnkey Construction

Complete execution oversight from foundation to handover using our verified network of builders and civil engineers.

Renovation & Interiors

Coordination of extensive renovations, modern modular kitchens, and premium interior finishes.

Transparent Pricing

Pricing is Project-based and quoted transparently only after a complete scope review.
