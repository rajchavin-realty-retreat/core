Construction Materials

The Marketplace
by Rajchavin.

A structured technology platform connecting customers with verified vendors for wholesale construction material supply. Zero markups. Pure facilitation.

Our Role in the Marketplace

In strict adherence to the Rajchavin UGRF, we maintain an absolute separation of roles. We are a technology facilitator, ensuring transparent pricing and smooth order tracking without acting as a direct seller.

Pure Facilitation

Rajchavin operates a technology marketplace facilitating transactions between customers and verified vendors. We do not own inventory.

Not a Direct Seller

Because we are not the seller, there are no hidden markups on the materials. You get direct pricing from the manufacturer or wholesale vendor.

Liability & Quality

Product liability, replacements, and returns rest solely with the vendor or manufacturer, ensuring accountability remains where it belongs.

Simple, Uniform Pricing

Our Master Pricing Policy ensures that there are no surprises when ordering construction materials through the Rajchavin Marketplace.

For Customers

FREE

As a promotional offer, platform charges for customers are currently completely free.

For Vendors

0.99%

Of the total bill value. We earn a strict, minimal platform fee only from the vendor side.
