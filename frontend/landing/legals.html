Risk Awareness

Before you register, ensure you have clear answers to these critical questions:

01.

Is the seller’s title clear for 20+ years?

02.

Are there any hidden mortgages or liens?

03.

Is the land conversion fully legal and documented?

04.

Are the EC records verified at the sub-registrar office?

05.

Is the stamp duty calculated correctly to avoid penalties?
