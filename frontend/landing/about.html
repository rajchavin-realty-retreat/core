Redefining Governance

About Rajchavin

Rajchavin is a next-generation Real Estate, Construction and Technology-enabled Advisory Group Built on one Uncompromising Principle: Long-Term Value Creation Through Trust, Governance and Execution Excellence.

Our Core Philosophy

We operate on absolute separation of Land, Money, and Systems. Our policies are designed to protect Customers, Developers, and Financial Institutions equally.

Success-Linked Earnings

We earn only when work progresses or completes. No hidden charges, and transparent invoicing in advance.

Zero Custody of Funds

Customer and developer money is never held by Rajchavin. All funds flow directly through authorized banking channels.

Clear Role Separation

Land, Money, and System remain strictly independent. We provide the governance while owners hold the assets.

Compliance-First

Bank-friendly, audit-ready, and regulator-safe. We ensure documented, traceable closures for every transaction.

What We Do

A complete ecosystem of services governed by strict protocols. From finding a plot to financing its construction.

Real Estate Transactions

End-to-end management for buying, selling, renting, and leasing. We handle residential, commercial, industrial properties, and plots.

  • Transparent 0.99% success fee logic
  • Buyer-Seller coordination & documentation tracking
  • Registration & auditable closure

Layouts & Farmlands

Project governance & sales enablement for layout developers and farmland owners (Sandalwood, Teak, Mango, etc.).

  • Project verification & ownership review
  • Sales readiness & layout structuring
  • Farmland ROI tracking & management logic

RaaS™ (Real Estate as a Service)

Our proprietary software platform for developers to sell plots safely and manage inventory seamlessly.

  • Basic: Software access & documentation guidance
  • Standard: Managed documentation & execution tracking
  • Premium & Elite: Full sales execution, bank coordination & scaling

Design & Construction

A strict governance & coordination model. We don't construct directly—we manage execution through verified professionals.

  • 2D Plans & 3D Elevations
  • Turnkey construction & renovations
  • Milestone-based payment oversight

Legals & Loans

Assistance and coordination via licensed professionals and bankers to ensure your assets are clean and funded.

  • Sale Deeds, Agreements & Legal Opinions
  • Khata, EC, RTC & NA Conversions
  • Home, Plot & Developer Loan facilitation

Material Marketplace

A technology marketplace connecting customers with verified vendors for wholesale construction material supply.

  • Cement, TMT, Plumbing & Electricals
  • Transparent billing & tracking
  • Free for customers (promotional)
